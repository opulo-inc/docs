# LumenPnP Version 4.2 Introduction

==================

## What's new?

### Tapped Metal Staging Plates

* Increases rigidity and improves strength
    <img src="../"/>
* Tapped M4 holes for easy mounting, and other possibilities.
* Backwards compatible with all M3 pass-through hardware, as they can slip through the tapped M4 opening.
* The metal improve rigidity of the entire machine.
* Because they are now made of metal, there's no more need for a bottom camera foot support, which should result in more consistent z-heights across the entire plate, no matter the table it is on.

### New Build Plates

* Uses entire available area between the front and back feeders.
* Two 75mm wide metal plates now come with the LumenPnP kit automatically.
* You have the option to purchase an additional 75mm plate to fully maximize the the working area that's available to you.
* The metal upgrade has also made the foot support for the build plates unnecessary, which should result in more consistent z-heights no matter the table it is on.

	<img src="images/

### New Bottom Camera & Homing Fiducial Location

* Increases build area to handle large board sizes that were never possible with prior LumenPnP versions without modification.
* Combines the primary and secondary fiducials into one mounted piece that are mounted at the appropriate difference for calibration.
* It is utilizes unused space by occupying the space between the bottom camera and the feeders.
* It holds the calibration disk used during initial setup and any recalibration, if needed.

	<img src="images/

### New Discard Bin

* It cuts down on wasting/losing parts.
* It has a dedicated location.

 	<img src="images/

* The cup is easily removed from its cradle to empty the bin
* It can Be Removed/Relocated to increase available space, or for personal preference.
